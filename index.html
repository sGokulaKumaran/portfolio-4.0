About

Crafting worlds with code

I'm a passionate Game & Full-Stack Developer focused on building immersive digital experiences and modern web solutions using Unity, C#, React, and JavaScript. I think of every project as a small world — it has its own physics, its own personality, and its own set of rules that someone (hopefully me) gets to invent.

Currently pursuing B.Tech in Computer Science & Business Systems at Ramco Institute of Technology — blending a business mindset with technical engineering. I work in the seam between disciplines: prototyping in code when that's faster than Figma, sitting in on research when the answer isn't obvious, shipping the actual production build instead of handing off a spec and hoping.

Remote-friendly, based in Tamil Nadu, India, and quietly optimistic about what the next browser can do.
